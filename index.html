Projects

Automatic AC Fan Regulation System Using Temperature Sensing

Final-year thesis, March to November 2025. Grade: A.

A closed-loop controller that regulates AC fan speed from temperature. An ATmega8 reads a DHT11 sensor and drives the fan through a TRIAC module. An opto-coupler triggers the TRIAC and detects each mains zero crossing, and I also worked with a photodiode in the circuit.

  • Response time of about 50 to 60 ms in bench tests.
  • 98.79% accuracy in matching fan speed to the temperature thresholds.
  • Presented at IECEC 2026, University of Nigeria, Nsukka, with Ogara David Nnamdi and Onah Valentine Onyedikachi. Supervisor: Engr. Dr. C. M. Ogbuka.
